Lever Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out mass times distance from the numbers you type. The masses, distances and beam are yours to measure or read off your own setup. It treats every mass as a point at its distance and every beam as straight, so it has not seen your setup and does not know the beam's strength or own weight, the pivot's friction, angles, movement or how things are rigged, and it gives no verdict that a lift, a beam or a rig is adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

The balance truth

Two masses on opposite sides of a pivot, each at its distance. Mass in kg, distance in m.

The match truth

The distance that makes side two balance side one, using the masses and the side one distance above.

The fulcrum truth

Where the pivot goes on a beam with the two masses at its ends.

Lever Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
